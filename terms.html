Using RelayAI means accepting what is on this page. The short version is pinned in our Discord server; this is the full one, including exactly how ranks, slots and the queue behave.

1. What RelayAI is

RelayAI is a Discord bot that completes online homework on your behalf, on accounts you provide. It currently works with Sparx Maths, Sparx Science, Sparx Reader, Seneca and Languagenut. Everything happens inside our Discord server — there is no app, extension or download, and this website exists only to manage your account.

We run it ourselves on our own hardware in the United Kingdom. It is not a registered company, and it is not connected to any of the platforms it works with.

2. Who can use it

  • You must meet Discord's own minimum age, which is 13 in the UK and higher in some countries. Accounts found to be underage are removed.
  • If you are under 18, you should have a parent or guardian's agreement to use it.
  • One person, one account. Do not share your rank or let other people use your access.

Read this part properly. Your school almost certainly considers this cheating. Using RelayAI can get you in trouble at school, and the homework platform can suspend or ban the account you use. Those are your risks to carry, not ours. If you are not willing to carry them, do not use RelayAI.

3. Accounts you give us

  • Only use logins that are yours, or that you have clear permission to use. Handing us someone else's account is the fastest way to lose access permanently.
  • You can save a login so you do not have to type it each time. Saved logins are encrypted with a master password only you know — the Privacy page explains what that means in practice.
  • You can save up to 25 accounts. Changing your master password destroys all of them, because without it they cannot be decrypted by anyone, us included.
  • A main account is your own account on a platform. Setting one is permanent, and work run on it does not use up a slot.

5. The free trial

The free trial gives you four full days: 96 hours of paid-level access from the moment it is activated, on as much homework as you like. It is one per person, it cannot be reactivated, and it expires by itself. Claiming repeat trials with extra accounts ends your access.

6. Plans and payment

  • Monthly — £2. One month of access. Bought by card, it renews every month until you cancel — use Manage billing on your account page, or your Link account. Cancelling stops the next charge; the month already paid for still runs out normally. Bought in Discord, it does not renew and simply lapses unless you buy again.
  • Lifetime — £10, once. Access for as long as RelayAI runs. That is not a promise it runs forever — see section 8.
  • Priority — £5, once. An add-on that stacks on Monthly or Lifetime, putting you at the front of the queue with 3 slots.
  • Unlimited accounts — £5, once. Removes the daily limit on how many school accounts you can run, for as long as RelayAI runs.
  • Reset slots — £0.99. Clears the school accounts you have used today so you can run more before the midnight reset. It only covers that day.

By card, on our pricing page. Payment is handled by Stripe and your purchase is sold through Link (link.com), Stripe's reseller, which adds any VAT due, emails your receipt and handles payment questions. Your statement will show LINK.COM. We never see or store your card details. Card purchases are applied automatically, usually within a couple of minutes; Monthly, Unlimited accounts and Reset slots need your Discord linked first.

In Discord, by opening an Upgrade ticket. We take gift cards and crypto. Staff apply your rank by hand once payment arrives — usually quickly, but not instantly and not at a guaranteed time of day.

Crypto payments cannot be reversed. Check the address and the amount before sending. The only wallet we collect to is the one published on our pricing page and posted by staff inside your own ticket. If anybody direct-messages you a different address, it is not us, and nothing sent there can be recovered.

7. Refunds

  • If you pay and your rank is never applied, you get a full refund.
  • If the service does not work for you and we cannot fix it, ask in a ticket and we will refund you.
  • Beyond that, we do not refund a plan you have already used, and "lifetime" means the life of the service.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid where possible: card refunds to the card, crypto refunds to the address you sent from.
  • A refunded or charged-back purchase is removed from your account.

8. Availability, and things ending

  • We do not promise uptime. It runs on our own hardware and can go down for maintenance, faults, or power and internet problems.
  • Homework platforms change constantly. If one changes how it works, support can break or stop with no notice, and we may retire a platform entirely.
  • RelayAI itself may stop one day. If that happens, unused time on a Monthly plan is refundable; Lifetime is not, which is the trade you make for paying once.

9. Accuracy is not guaranteed

We aim for correct answers, and most come from a database of work that has already been done and checked. Nothing is perfect. Answers can be wrong, and a task can stop part-finished if a platform changes mid-run. Check the work before you submit it. Your marks are your responsibility.

  • Claiming repeat free trials, or evading a removal with new accounts.

Any of those can mean suspension or permanent removal, without notice and without refund.
