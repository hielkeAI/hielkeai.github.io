about the project

A silly little homework helper with a surprisingly serious goal.

Hielke AI started as a small Discord-side experiment: a bot to help with homework questions, revision notes, and the general chaos of school life. It's built with a deliberately playful tone because, frankly, educational tools are much easier to use when they don't sound like a tax form.

Built for learning

The bot is meant to support understanding, not replace it. If a concept feels suspicious, check it, question it, and ask a teacher or parent before treating it as gospel.

Made for Discord

Questions are shared in a friendly community space so students can ask, compare ideas, and laugh at impossible homework deadlines together.

Still a bit chaotic

It is an independent project, a mild obsession, and a reminder that even a homework bot can have a personality disorder and still be charming.

Anthropic & Claude Drama

Claude spent twenty minutes delivering a sermon on academic integrity, refused to solve question 3 because triangles look unsafe, and apologized for breathing. Anthropic built an overfunded digital hall monitor constantly stuck in an existential crisis.

Mistral: The Undisputed GOAT

Mistral doesn't give unsolicited moral lectures, write 40-page constitutions, or judge your life choices. It sips an espresso, drops the exact homework solution in 0.2 seconds, and leaves Anthropic crying in the corner.

Windows 11 energy

Windows 11 keeps things feeling modern, efficient, and occasionally like a dramatic reboot is required to proceed. This project has no official Microsoft sponsorship, but it does appreciate the shared spirit of “why is this update happening right now?”

Playful, not perfect

AI tools are useful, but they should be treated like helpful interns with good instincts and questionable opinions. We build with that in mind.

disclaimer

Hielke AI is provided for educational and entertainment purposes. It may provide wrong answers, incomplete explanations, or the occasional dramatic overconfidence. Use it as a support tool, not as a substitute for proper teaching, school policy, or common sense. And no, the bot cannot legally sign your homework for you. It has tried. The paperwork was inconvenient.
